Pilgrim Fares

A flight-deal tracker for Catholic pilgrimage sites. Watch destinations like Fatima, Lourdes, and the Holy Land, and it ranks fares by how far under the usual price they are, how many stops they have, the airline, and whether the trip lands on a feast day. The real version checks live fares every day and emails an alert. I built it for my dad, who is its only user.

Demo. The fares below are generated sample data, so none of them can be booked. The scoring rule and the feast-day dates (including movable feasts like Easter and Divine Mercy Sunday) are computed the way the app does it. Nothing you click leaves your browser.
