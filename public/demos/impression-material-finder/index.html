Impression Material Finder

Pick what matters most for the case. The tool ranks hundreds of dental impression materials, burs, and cements by those priorities and lets you filter by manufacturer, type, and set time. I built it with our prosthodontics clinic’s dental assistant to replace a lot of spec-sheet hunting.

Prototype. This is a public copy running on a sample catalog in your browser. Specs are representative of published manufacturer data, so confirm against the technical data sheet before ordering. Prices are list estimates and any you edit here are not saved.

How the ranking works: each priority you pick is scored 0 to 100 against the products currently showing, then blended with the first pick weighted most. The best product for a priority always scores 100 on it, so the list reshuffles as you filter.
